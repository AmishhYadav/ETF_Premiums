# Valve status per ETF-month

Generated by `uv run etfp export valve-status` from the `valve_monthly` table built by `uv run etfp build` (units data through 2026-11-16). Do not edit by hand; rebuild and regenerate. Rule: DECISIONS D33 and `src/etfp/features/valve.py`.

Legend: **O** open (units rose: a creation happened) · **·** closed (data present, units did not move) · **R** redeeming only (units fell, never rose) · blank unknown (no units data, nothing to compare with, or NSE refreshed no fund's count that month). `closed` means *no creation happened*; for a fund at a large premium that is the evidence the valve was shut, but a fund with no premium can be `closed` just because nobody asked.

Two sources. From 5 Feb 2024 the NSE security master (`IssdCptl`, daily files); before that, and in months when NSE's file says nothing, the AMC's own monthly portfolio (net assets / AMFI NAV, D46). Monthly rule (D48): a month counts as open or redeeming when units moved by at least 0.9 of a creation basket (`config/etfs.yaml`); smaller moves are rounding and closed. Where the AMFI NAV used is dated differently from the month-end (weekends, a day's lag) the estimate carries that day's price move, so `status_rule` ends `_timing` and the month should be trusted only if the change persists next month (a 'creation' that reverses the month after is a timing artefact). Months with no such flag reproduce whole baskets to within 0.02 basket.

NSE refreshes units outstanding (`IssdCptl`) in batches about once a week, for all funds at once, not daily. A month's status is therefore dated by the batch that published the change: a creation in the last days of a month can show in the next month. No batch at all ran between 5 Feb and 14 Mar 2024, so for Feb and Mar 2024 the AMC figures are used instead (the 14 Mar batch published February's changes).
